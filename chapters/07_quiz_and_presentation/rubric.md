# Presentation and Interview Rubric

Score each category from 1 to 5.

| Category | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Technical accuracy | Major errors | Mostly correct | Precise, correct, handles nuance |
| Structure | Hard to follow | Logical | Clear narrative with strong transitions |
| Business connection | Metrics only | Some outcomes | Decision, cost, capacity, risk, and value connected |
| Leadership judgment | No governance | Mentions controls | Owners, stages, guardrails, fallback, and rollback |
| Communication | Reads notes | Mostly conversational | Concise, confident, audience-aware |
| Limitations | None | General caveats | Specific limitations and mitigation |
| Follow-up answers | Avoids or rambles | Adequate | Direct, evidence-based, acknowledges uncertainty |

## Passing score

- Minimum total: 28 out of 35.
- No score below 3 in technical accuracy, leadership judgment, or limitations.
